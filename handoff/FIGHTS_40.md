- Note: the `flow/KF_F01…KF_F27` keyframes in the repo belong to UNCROWNED-3 and are **not** these F01–F40 shots.

**Rules built into every prompt:** no music, no score, no Seedance soundtrack (SFX + lines only) · powers are physical only (shockwave as air distortion, dust rings, cracked marble, hanging raindrops); no neon, beams, sparkles or anime lines · real light source named, natural sky, no fake god-rays · anamorphic 35 mm, film grain · faces and wardrobe locked to refs, nobody looks like a real actor · every strike lands on a body, guard or object (F40 ends on purpose one frame before contact; the cut to black happens in the edit).
